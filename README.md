# Wanderlust

Wanderlust is an AI-powered travel planner that helps users create smart trip itineraries, browse ideas, and explore destinations with rich visuals.

## How to Use

- Open the app and go to the trip planning page.
- Enter your travel details such as:
  - Destination or city
  - Trip duration (number of days)
  - Interests (for example: adventure, food, nature, culture, shopping)
  - Budget style (budget, moderate, or premium-style travel)
  - Any optional preferences you want the AI to consider
- Click generate to create your AI itinerary.
- Review the plan day by day:
  - Morning, afternoon, and evening suggestions
  - Activity sequencing for a smoother travel flow
  - Destination highlights and must-visit spots
- If the result is not exactly what you want, adjust your inputs and regenerate.
- Save time by browsing recent itineraries and reusing ideas for your next trip.

## Features

- **AI itinerary generation**  
  Generates personalized plans based on your destination, duration, and preferences.

- **Day-wise planning**  
  Organizes your trip into clear day-by-day plans so you can follow a structured schedule.

- **Preference-based recommendations**  
  Adapts suggestions to match your interests and travel style instead of giving generic plans.

- **Flexible regeneration**  
  Lets you quickly regenerate plans with updated inputs to compare alternatives and pick the best one.

- **Recent itinerary exploration**  
  Shows previously generated itineraries so users can discover ideas and plan faster.

- **Visual destination discovery**  
  Uses rich destination imagery to make planning more engaging and help users choose places confidently.

- **Simple planning workflow**  
  Keeps the process easy: input details, generate plan, review, and refine.
